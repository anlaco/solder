Solder panel styles

A proposal for the Front Panel in the Solder theme (DT-056 §7): LabVIEW's controls, in the same places and sizes, drawn with the pattern shared by today's most liked interfaces. It runs: turn the Setpoint knob, drag the slider, flip the fan, press STOP.

The pattern

  • A quiet base, color only for meaningNeutral warm surfaces; one accent for what you act on, amber and red only for warnings and alarms (ISA-101 high-performance HMI, Grafana thresholds).
  • Depth on what you touch, flat on what you readKnobs, switches and buttons have a soft volume and a shadow; plots, labels and the panel stay flat (the 2025–26 return of tactile skeuomorphism, Teenage Engineering, Liquid Glass used sparingly).
  • Value as an arc or a fillA knob or gauge shows its value as a colored arc on a track, readable at a glance; the pointer only confirms it (FabFilter, Grafana gauges).
  • Numbers in monospace, tabular figuresValues never jump while they change; units in the interface font, muted (Geist Mono, TE).
  • Hairline rings instead of bordersA 1 px shadow ring at low opacity outlines surfaces without weight (Vercel Geist); radii of 7–10 px.
  • Controls raised, indicators recessedLabVIEW's own rule — white control, grey indicator — said with depth instead of color.
  • Light as feedbackLEDs glow, the focused field gets a soft accent halo, the needle eases into place.
  • The same HTML, any lookThe two styles above are only two sets of CSS tokens over the same markup: the panel style is a stylesheet.
